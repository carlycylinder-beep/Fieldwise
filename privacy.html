Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

The Fieldwise Group, LLC ("Fieldwise," "we," "us," or "our") operates thefieldwisegroup.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit the Site, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We collect limited information in two ways:

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site, including pages viewed, time on site, general location at the city or region level, device and browser type, and how you arrived at the Site. This data is aggregated and is not tied to your name unless you separately contact us.
  • Information you provide. If you email us directly at hello@thefieldwisegroup.com or book a call through our scheduling tool, we receive whatever information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, and the details of your inquiry.

2. Cookies

The Site uses basic cookies placed by Google Analytics to collect the analytics data described above. These cookies do not collect sensitive personal information. You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings, and most browsers let you block them. You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically using Google's browser add-on, available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics (analytics.google.com), to understand Site usage.
  • Calendly (calendly.com), to let you schedule a call with us. If you book a call, Calendly collects the scheduling information you provide directly.

We do not control these third parties' data practices and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to understand and improve how the Site is used, respond to inquiries and schedule calls, and communicate with current and prospective clients and program participants. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with service providers, such as Google Analytics or Calendly, solely to operate the Site, and we may disclose information if required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for business owners, operators, and adult professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your Choices

You can decline or clear cookies through your browser, opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the tool linked above, or contact us to ask what information, if any, we have associated with your email address.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The Effective Date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
